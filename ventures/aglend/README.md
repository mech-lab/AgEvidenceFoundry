# AgLend Company Pack

AgLend configures AgEvidence for agricultural lending evidence above the trust substrate.

It owns borrower, farm entity, underwriting profile, financial evidence, production evidence, risk condition, credit condition, and portfolio monitoring semantics.

It does not add credit scores, loan approvals, probability of default models, loss-given-default models, or lending decisions to AgEvidence core.

The product pack lives in `product/`. Commercial operating files live in `brand/`, `gtm/`, `accounts/`, `pitch/`, `research/`, and `formation/`.
