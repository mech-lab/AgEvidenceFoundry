# FieldProof Company Pack

FieldProof configures AgEvidence for field-trial and agtech efficacy reliance.

It owns trial, treatment, control, site, season, measurement, protocol, claim, efficacy threshold, and commercial determination semantics.

The product pack lives in `product/`. Commercial operating files live in `brand/`, `gtm/`, `accounts/`, `pitch/`, `research/`, and `formation/`.
