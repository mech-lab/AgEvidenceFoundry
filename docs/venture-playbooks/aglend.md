# AgLend

Company Pack: `ventures/aglend/company.yml`

Product Venture Pack: `ventures/aglend/product/venture.yml`

AgLend specializes AgEvidence above the trust substrate for agricultural lending evidence. The pack owns borrower vocabulary, farm entity evidence, underwriting profiles, financial evidence, production evidence, risk conditions, credit conditions, portfolio monitoring, and lender evidence packages.

Credit scoring, probability of default models, loss-given-default models, and lending decisions stay out of AgEvidence core.

Reliance gate: lender underwriting use.

Run:

```sh
bin/factory doctor aglend
bin/factory demo aglend
```
