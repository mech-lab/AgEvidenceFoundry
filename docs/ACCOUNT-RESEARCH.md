# Account Research

Commercial research follows evidence discipline.

Every external or internal assertion should carry:

- statement
- source
- confidence
- observation date
- fact or inference class
- owner when known
- expiry

Shared vocabulary lives under `commercial/vocabulary/`. Venture-level sources live under `ventures/<venture>/research/`.

Run:

```sh
bin/factory research stale methaneproof
```

Evidence classes:

- `verified_public_signal`
- `brief_derived`
- `supplied_research`
- `gtm_inference`

GTM inferences guide discovery and must not be represented externally as verified facts.
